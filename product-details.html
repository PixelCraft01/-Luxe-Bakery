About This Product

Our Butter Croissant is the cornerstone of LUXE Bakery, a testament to our unwavering commitment to quality and tradition. Each croissant begins its journey as a carefully portioned block of premium French butter, sourced directly from a single-origin creamery in Normandy, France.

Over the course of 72 hours, our master bakers perform a meticulous lamination process, folding the butter into layers of our proprietary dough blend. This time-intensive technique creates the signature honeycomb interior and shatteringly crisp exterior that our croissants are known for.

Before dawn each morning, our bakers shape and proof the croissants, allowing them to rise slowly in a temperature-controlled environment. The result is a perfectly golden, airy pastry that delivers an unparalleled buttery experience from the first flake to the last crumb.

Whether enjoyed plain, with a dollop of house-made jam, or alongside your morning espresso, our Butter Croissant elevates an ordinary moment into something truly special.
